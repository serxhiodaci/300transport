Our Equipment

Trailers

Whatever the freight, we have the right trailer for it.

Conestoga

ModelReitnouer
Year2020–2023
Length53'
Width103"

For customers with delicate, sensitive, or high-value freight that must stay weather-protected but can't be hauled on a Dry Van, we run brand-new custom-made Conestoga trailers. A Conestoga is a flatbed with a built-in sliding tarp system — once the load is secured, the driver simply slides the tarp cover into place in seconds, saving time during loading and unloading for everyone involved.

Our Conestoga fleet is custom-made, fully retractable, and low-profile: 53' long with a 103" interior height and width, built from full aluminum and able to open from both the front and rear for maximum loading convenience.

Dry Van

ModelUtility Trailer
Year2020–2023
Length53'
Width102"

Dry van shipping is the most common method for transporting freight across the interstate system. A dry van is a fully enclosed trailer that shields cargo from the elements — the go-to option for palletized and boxed freight.

Our Dry Van trailers are 53' long, 102" wide, and 110" tall — plated Utility trailers kept in rotation on a strict maintenance schedule.

Refrigerated trailers are the solution for transporting food and products that require temperature control from pickup to delivery. Our reefer trailers are state-of-the-art Utility trailers running Thermo King units.

We know how critical temp-controlled shipments are, which is why we've invested heavily in this fleet — including drop-trailer service, same-day shipping, and multi-drop capability.
